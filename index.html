About Us

Rooted in Jane Furse, ready to travel

Bars Graphics is a media company based in Jane Furse, Limpopo, built around one idea: the moments people gather for — weddings, parties, funerals and more — deserve to be captured properly and handed back to them without hassle.

We handle the photography, the video and the editing ourselves, so you deal with one team from booking to delivery. While we're proudly Limpopo-based, we take on events across the province and beyond.
